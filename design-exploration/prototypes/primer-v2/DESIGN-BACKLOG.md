## Keys

**Priority**
- **P0**: blocks Flutter implementation. Needs an owner decision, or it is a
  functional/data-fidelity discrepancy.
- **P1**: required before release (accessibility, missing designs, device or
  real-data validation).
- **P2**: visual quality; can wait for final polish.
- **P3**: housekeeping or minor.

**Status:** Open · Pending decision · Blocked on data · Needs validation ·
Accepted pending validation.

**Phase**
- **A** Before implementation: decision.
- **B** Before implementation: design work.
- **C** During implementation: verification in Flutter.
- **D** Device validation.
- **E** Data-dependent: needs real data or an existing complete fixture.
- **F** Final visual polish.
- **G** Housekeeping.
