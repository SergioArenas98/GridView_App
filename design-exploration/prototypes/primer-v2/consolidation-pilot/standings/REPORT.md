here.** The policies in [`../../CORE-DESIGN-DECISIONS.md`](../../CORE-DESIGN-DECISIONS.md)
stay pending, and no backlog item is closed. The approved `standings/`
prototype is unmodified. Method and background are in the Calendar pilot

(168 screenshot pairs) under deterministic rendering. DOM, computed styles,
boxes, accessibility trees, focus order, selector behaviour and scroll
restoration are identical too. The Calendar pilot still passes on the
refactored tooling.

**Notice handling.** Standings keeps `text-wrap: pretty` through a local
extension that loads after the shared notice: `.notice { text-wrap: pretty; }`
in `standings.css`. The shared file and Calendar are unchanged, and the
divergence stays documented as unresolved in the `notice.css` header. The

Static check: merging every selector's declarations in cascade order gives the
same result for all 130 selectors the pilot loads. The only absences are the 7
core rules for components Standings never uses (`section-title`, `tag`,
`text-link`, none in the markup).

## 4. Deterministic regression results

Utility: `../tools/visual-compare.mjs standings.config.mjs` uses Chrome
157.0.8103.38, software raster and compositing, DPR 2, mobile emulation with
touch, one session, baseline then pilot per run, and stable-frame capture.

**Runs (39):**

- **Static (24):** EN/ES × Drivers / Constructors × 390×844 / 320×693 ×
  100% / 200%. The fixture galleries are also captured as full-page segments
  at the same 4 sizes, in EN and ES. They cover the confirmed leader, the
  unranked "—" row, tied leaders, delivered order, missing name and team,
  decimal points 0.5 / 0,5, zero wins, uniform and mixed provisional,
  "Refreshing…", "Last successful check: 09:05" with the stale and failed
  notices, and empty / error / season unavailable / loading.
- **Interaction (15):** 8 keyboard focus walks, 2 forced-state runs, 4
  selector scenarios and 1 reduced-motion run (details in §5).

| Run | Screenshot pairs | Differing pixels | DOM / style / box / metric diffs | A11y-tree diffs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sw-pilot` (pilot vs approved) | 168 | **0** | 0 | 0 |
| `sw-control` (approved vs itself) | 168 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Each run compared 140 elements (291 on the fixture pages), including their
attributes, so `aria-pressed`, `hidden` and `data-fit` are covered.

**Measured in both versions, identical, with the pilot's values:**

- **Horizontal scroll and overflow.** No horizontal scroll in any run; 0
  elements outside the viewport; 0 clipped, spilling or under-12.5px text.
- **Mid-word breaks: 0 in app copy.** The fixture pages break 1–10 words, all
  in development-only captions: file paths and "StandingsFirstLoadError:" in
  `.spec__src` (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), and "section-level" at its hyphen.
  The baseline breaks exactly the same words.
- **Touch targets: 0 under 48×48 on the app pages.** The only small target is
  the fixture page's "Back to the prototype" annotation link (131×24 /
  263×41). It is identical in the baseline and is not app UI.
- **Contrast.** Minimum 7.02:1 on flat backgrounds (7.44 on the fixture
  pages). Text on the Chalk leader gradient and on the oxide selector slab is
  listed as complex, and the pixel equality covers it.
- **Title-to-actions gap:** 124.77 / 54.77 / 67.58 / 25.13 (EN 390, 320, 390
  at 200%, 320 at 200%). Spanish: 52.73 / 13.13 / 8.59 / 7.58, with the title
  at `wdth` 100, or scaled to 32.75px at 320 @200%. These match the approved
  README's validation table (125 / 55 / 68 / 25 / 53 / 13 / 9 / 8).
- **Selected nav label.** Spanish at normal text and every 200% case drop to
  `wdth` 62 / 700 (condensed or stacked nav). English at normal text keeps
  `wdth` 100 / 800 (regular nav). The label fits every time.
- **Header budget.** The app bar row scrolls away at 320 @200% and ES 390
  @200%; it stays pinned elsewhere. The selector fit is regular, condensed or
  stacked exactly as in the baseline.

**GPU raster** was not used for Standings. The Calendar pilot showed it varies
between page loads even with no CSS change.

## 5. Accessibility and interaction results

- **Accessibility trees identical** in every run, including leader, tied and
  provisional row labels, `aria-pressed`, the "Championship" group and the
  status regions. Trees are saved per page and view in `sw-pilot/`.
- **Keyboard order identical:** 8 walks, 13 stops on Drivers and 9 on
  Constructors (the hidden table is skipped), with matching focus-ring pixels.
- **Forced `:hover` / `:active`** on the actions, unselected segment, leader
  row, ordinary row and nav tab: pixel-identical.
- **Selector and scroll restoration** (EN/ES, 390@100 and 320@200). These six
  assertions hold in baseline and pilot alike:
  - the first switch opens Constructors at the top, with Drivers hidden;
  - a repeated tap changes nothing;
  - Drivers' offset is restored (400 → Constructors → 400);
  - Constructors' offset is restored;
  - Enter selects;
  - Space selects.

  The recorded states are identical. Limit: Constructors has one confirmed
  row and cannot scroll at these sizes, so its restoration is checked at
  offset 0 only.
- **Reduced motion:** computed styles identical.

## 6. Newly found shared-system inconsistencies

None of these is in the backlog. They were not added there and are reported
here for the owner to decide whether to track them (they fit under XS-09):

1. **Empty-state sizes:** list screens (Standings, Explore) use a 32px icon
   and a 20px title; detail screens (GP, Driver, Team, Circuit) use 28px and
   18px. It may be an intended density difference, but no decision records it.
2. **Selector padding:** `8px 12px` in Standings, `8px 10px` in Explore.
   Possibly deliberate for three segments; not documented.
3. **Pinned header and nav fallback** are already identical in Standings and
   Explore. They are ready to share once S-1/S-2/S-3 (XS-06, XS-07, XS-08)
   are decided, which would also settle Calendar's sticky variant.

## 8. Ready for a third screen?

**Yes, with one condition.** The foundation absorbed a second screen with a
new shared component, a local extension, a deeper folder and a generated
page, at zero difference. The utility now takes a config, so a third screen
needs only a config file.

The condition: the next screens bring components whose copies disagree. Those
need a decision, or the local-extension pattern, before extraction:

- the detail app bar (4 identical copies plus 2 variants);
- chips (the GP additions);
- the empty-state sizes;
- the pinned header (policy pending).

**Recommended next target: Explore** (Alternative B on the `explore/`
baseline). It shares the identical pinned header, nav fallback and empty
state with Standings, so it tests:

- extracting those once their status allows, or keeping them as local
  extensions until then;
- the selector with a real variant (3 columns, 10px padding) on a shared
  base;
- the notice with `text-wrap: pretty`;
- a second folder level (`comparison-missing-media/`).

The detail screens (GP, Driver, Team, Circuit) would follow, with the detail
app bar as their first new shared component.

Nothing beyond Standings was migrated.
