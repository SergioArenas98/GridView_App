## 4. A1: solid outline

**Computed**, Chalk-alpha composited on each surface:

| Surface | 22% (current) | **45% (candidate)** | 60% (dashed, alert) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground `#141518` | 1.87 | **3.96** | 6.08 |
| tray `#1F2125` | 1.91 | **3.83** | 5.70 |
| raised `#2A2D32` | 1.89 | **3.59** | 5.18 |

| Surface | on-chalk 35% (current) | **on-chalk 55% (candidate)** |
|---|---|---|
| Chalk `#ECEAE5` | 2.19 | **3.84** |
| chalk-low `#E2DFD8` | 2.16 | **3.72** |

**Rendered**: the border pixel against the pixel just outside it, at 390 EN.

| Chip | Current | Candidate |
|---|---|---|
| DNF, DNS, DSQ, DNQ, Lapped, Provisional, Not available (ground) | `#434344` / `#141518` = 1.85 | `#757574` = **3.96** |
| Upcoming, Completed (GP header surface `#26282C`) | 1.89 | **3.71** |
| Standings marker on the Chalk leader row | `#9D9B98` / `#E6E4DE` = 2.18 | `#737371` = **3.74** |
| Dashed "Status unknown" (reference, unchanged) | 5.37–6.02 | 5.37–6.02 |
| Solid alert "Cancelled", "Postponed" (unchanged) | 6.04 | 6.04 |

**Role.** The outline is not what identifies these chips. The word does, so
1.4.11 does not strictly require 3:1 here, and **the text is not used as
evidence of compliance**. What the outline carries is the VS-1 shape
vocabulary: solid for known values, dashed for unknown ones. At 22% that
shape was close to invisible (1.85–1.91), so unknown read louder than known.
At 45% the solid shape is perceivable above 3:1 on every measured surface.

The hierarchy is kept: known chips at 45% sit below unknown and alert chips at
60%, and the dashed pattern still differs from the solid one. In the crops the
secondary chips gain a visible edge without competing with the Final chip or
the row text.

**One role, two surface values.** A Chalk alpha is invisible on Chalk, so the
inverse row needs the on-chalk value. This is the same role mapped per
surface, which is also what the light theme will need.

**Caveat.** The GP pilot still renders the finish "Status unknown" as a solid
chip, because VS-1's dashed treatment has not been applied to the pilots. The
candidate therefore strengthens it too (frame 22). In implementation it must
take the dashed border.

## 5. A2: selected fill

| Pair | Gradient (current) | Flat oxide (candidate) |
|---|---|---|
| Slab vs tray (selector container) | 2.89 (`#B04633`) → 2.47 (oxide) → **1.72** (`oxide-deep`) | **2.47** uniform |
| Chalk label on the slab | 4.63 (`#B04633`) to 7.80 (`oxide-deep`) | **5.43** uniform |
| Unselected label `text-2` on tray | 7.02 | 7.02 |
| Selected vs unselected label colour (Chalk vs `text-2`) | 1.91 | 1.91 |
| Calendar block vs band `#1B1D20` / tray | 2.59–2.89, down to 1.72 | 2.59 / 2.47 |
| Chalk number on the Calendar block | 4.63–7.80 | 5.43 |

Flat oxide removes `#B04633`, which is not a token. It raises the worst case
of the slab against the tray from 1.72 to 2.47, and gives the label one
uniform 5.43:1. In the crops the change is subtle: the slab simply loses its
shading.

**It does not make the slab a sufficient state indicator on its own.** At
2.47:1 against the tray it stays below 3:1, and no fill on the tray can reach
3:1 while keeping a Chalk label at 4.5:1. The existing cues are:

- weight 600 → 800, the only cue that does not depend on colour;
- the label colour change, which is only 1.91:1 and is itself a colour cue;
- `aria-pressed`, which is programmatic and has no visible form.

Several cues being present does not establish compliance.

**Smallest added indicator tested (`-mark`).** A Chalk underline on the
selected label, at 5.43:1 on oxide, is a shape cue that does not depend on
colour.

- **What works:** it is part of the text box, so it causes no layout change
  (0 box differences). It follows the wrapped label when the selector stacks
  at 320 @200%, in both EN and ES, and it stays clear of the focus ring.
- **What is wrong with it:** Primer already underlines links (the GP team
  links "Red Bull Racing", "McLaren"…), so a selected segment may read as a
  link. That is a vocabulary collision for the owner to judge.

A short Chalk 12° tick, from Primer's seam vocabulary, would avoid the
collision but was not tested.

**Selection behaviour is unchanged:** the same buttons, `aria-pressed`, click
handler and fit script.

## 6. A3: focus rings

Method: each Tab stop is captured focused and unfocused at the same scroll
position.

- **Ring pixels** are the pixels that change between the two captures.
- **Ring colour** is the most frequent changed colour.
- **Adjacent** means every unchanged pixel touching the ring, i.e. the pixels
  next to its actual rendered boundary. The table gives their minimum ratio
  against the ring colour, and the share of them at 3:1 or more.
- **Changed ≥3** is the share of ring pixels that differ from their own
  unfocused colour by at least 3:1.

| Stop (390 EN) | Current | Candidate |
|---|---|---|
| **Selected** selector button | Ember on slab: min **2.27**, p05 2.31, **29%** adjacent ≥ 3:1, changed ≥ 3: 28% | Chalk: min **5.43**, **100%**, changed ≥ 3: 97% |
| Selected button, stacked 320 @200% EN/ES | min 2.27, 26% | min 5.43, 100% |
| **Unselected** selector button (Ember on tray) | min 2.61, 95% | min 2.66, **92%** (see below) |
| Unselected button, stacked 320 @200% | 6.57, 100% | 6.57, 100% |
| **Standings leader row** | oxide ring outside the row, **on graphite**: **2.80, 0%** | Ember: **7.44, 100%** |
| Leader rows in `standings/states` (7 stops, 320 @200%) | 2.80, 0% | 7.44, 100% |
| **Calendar next-event** link | Ember: min 2.54 where it meets the oxide block (rest 6.57) | Chalk: min **5.43** (rest 13.41) |
| Ordinary Calendar row (Ember on graphite) | 5.52–7.44, 100% | unchanged |

**Corrections and limits found by measuring:**

1. **Standings leader row.** Step 51A listed this ring as "oxide on Chalk,
   5.43, met". That was wrong. The ring is offset 2px **outside** the Chalk
   row, so it is drawn on graphite at 2.80:1 and fails everywhere.

   The candidate applies the per-surface rule (Ember on graphite, 7.44). This
   is a colour change only. Home's `.champ :focus-visible` (also oxide) was
   not measured; it should be checked with the same method.

2. **Calendar next-event.** In both versions the oxide block paints **over**
   the inset ring, so the ring is interrupted at the top left. Only its ends
   touch the block. Chalk raises those ends from 2.54 to 5.43, but the gap
   remains (`A3-cal-next-focus-*`). Closing it would need a stacking or
   offset change, which was not tested.

3. **Unselected selector button, regular layout.** The selected slab's 12°
   tip extends into the neighbouring button, and the unselected button's
   Ember ring crosses it at 2.66:1. That is 5% of adjacent samples on the
   gradient and 8% on flat oxide (flat oxide makes the tip uniformly dark).
   **The candidate does not resolve this.** The simplest option is a Chalk
   ring on every selector button (13.41 on tray, 5.43 on oxide). It was not
   tested visually.

**Selected vs focused vs both.**

| State | What shows |
|---|---|
| Selected | Slab, plus Chalk 800 label (plus the underline on `-mark`) |
| Focused, unselected | Ember ring on the tray, label `text-2` 600, no slab |
| Focused and selected | The slab plus a full Chalk ring inside it |

The ring is a perimeter outline and the underline is a short bar under the
text, so they do not merge. Disabled is untested: no disabled control exists
on these pages.

## 11. Limits

- This is Chrome emulation. The 200% case is a root font-size, not Android 14
  non-linear scaling. System insets are zero.
- Contrast figures are sRGB pixel values at DPR 2 under software raster. At
  DPR 1 a 1.5px border is antialiased, so it renders lighter on dark, and the
  margin above 3:1 matters.
- None of this is TalkBack, D-pad or device evidence (XS-02, XS-05, XS-08,
  XS-16).
- Only the screens listed were tested. Home, Explore, Driver, Team, Circuit
  and Settings rings were not measured.

### 12.2 Selection cue: flat slab (A) vs 12° cue (B)

Option B is a 3px Chalk bar cut at `var(--cut)`, the geometry of the
Standings leader seam and the team tick. It sits in the selected slab's
leading padding, with no new token, no icon and no underline.

- It measures 5.43:1 on flat oxide. It is 22px tall at 390 and 26px at
  320 @200%.
- It is 20–82px from the label text, and 6.2–6.7px clear of the focus ring.
- Button heights stay 48 / 52px. Fit modes are unchanged after switching the
  selection (regular at 390; stacked at 320 @200%, EN and ES).

**Assessment of the whole state treatment (A).** The selected option is a
large flat oxide block against the tray, with a Chalk 800 label against a
`text-2` 600 label, plus `aria-pressed`.

- The block's luminance step is 2.47:1, below 3:1.
- With colour removed (Chrome's achromatopsia emulation,
  `C1-selector-flat-achromatopsia-en-390.png`), the block still reads as a
  clearly lighter, filled segment, and the label weight changes with it.
- The selected state therefore does not depend on hue. It does depend on a
  luminance difference below 3:1, paired with weight.

**B adds a ≥ 3:1 mark that does not depend on colour, but it does not read
clearly as a selection cue.** At both sizes it looks like a stray slash before
the label ("/ Drivers", "/ Pilotos"), a typographic character rather than a
state. In grayscale it adds little to the already visible block.

**Recommendation: keep the simpler flat slab (A) without the cue.** Record
the selected-state rationale as: filled slab, plus weight 600 → 800, plus
label colour, plus `aria-pressed` / selected semantics.

**Remaining uncertainty:**
- A strict reading of WCAG 1.4.11 could still ask for a 3:1 state boundary,
  and the slab is 2.47:1.
- How the state reads with TalkBack, and on OLED panels at system font
  scale, is unverified (XS-02, XS-16).
